## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial-first retail identity for high-velocity Pakistani fashion e-commerce. It bridges regional South Asian sartorial heritage—spanning ready-to-wear pret, unstitched multi-piece textiles, and formal eastern silhouettes—with the restraint and typographic authority of contemporary luxury fashion publications.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with high-conversion e-commerce functionalism:
- **Purity and Contrast:** Canvas elements defer completely to campaign photography. Bold stark black `#000000` grounds structural navigation, CTAs, and authoritative footers, balanced against warm stone and ecru micro-surfaces that soften raw digital white.
- **Editorial Polish:** High-fashion storytelling phrases interlock crisp geometric sans-serif clarity with italicized, literary serif accents (e.g. *Style that moves with you*), elevating commercial mass-retail into an aspirational cultural experience.
- **Commercial Precision:** Scarcity chips (`BEST SELLER`, `LIMITED`, `SELLING FAST`, `LOW STOCK`) and local currency pricing (`Rs.`) are treated with mathematical discipline—micro-scale, high-contrast, and strictly non-intrusive.
- **Audience & Emotion:** Targeted at trend-discerning Pakistani and global diaspora consumers seeking accessible luxury, reliable execution, and everyday elegance. The UI communicates credibility, effortless refinement, and instant tactile clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in high-contrast editorial monochrome, using warmth in secondary neutrals to complement South Asian skin tones and rich textile dyes without color contamination.

### Application Rules
- **Primary (`#000000`):** Reserved for primary CTA buttons, site header brand mark, critical headings, and the structured black enterprise footer.
- **Secondary (`#222222`):** Primary text color for body copy, category links, product titles, and active tab indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#767676`):** Supporting metadata, overline eyebrows, inactive navigation links, review counts, and service icons.
- **Neutral (`#F7F6F2` & `#EDEBE7`):** Warm ecru and stone backdrops for social proof callout blocks, campaign banner panels, metric grids, and secondary card chip states.
- **Card Badges:** 
  - *High-urgency/Stock cues (`BEST SELLER`, `LIMITED`, `LOW STOCK`):* Translucent stark charcoal overlay `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` with `#FFFFFF` text anchored directly within top card bounds.
  - *Product Attribute cues (`MODERN FIT`, `SEASONAL WEAVES`):* Subtle `#F3F3F3` backdrop with `#222222` lettering.

## Typography

The typographic architecture establishes an elevated balance between structural utility and high-fashion editorial sensitivity.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Workhorse Engine:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers crisp legibility across high-density merchandising grids, mega-menus, pricing matrices, and technical sizing guides.
- **Editorial Flourish:** `Newsreader` (italic or normal weights) is integrated as a secondary display accent for storytelling statements, campaign catchphrases (e.g., *“Style that moves with you”*), and curated capsule intros.

### Hierarchy & Style Details
- **Eyebrow & Badges:** Rendered in strict uppercase tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) with bold weights to maintain sharp clarity even at compact sizes (`9px` to `11px`).
- **Product Titles:** Set at `14px` or `12px` in clean regular-to-medium sans, letting the photography lead without cognitive visual clutter.
- **Pricing:** Rendered in bold monospace-like clarity `Rs. 2,190` with strict currency baseline alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column layout system designed to accommodate high-density fashion catalogs while maintaining editorial negative space.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Product cards render in 2-column or horizontal scroll strips.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product listings render in 3-column rows.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 3.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, maximum container constraint of `1440px`. Product grids render in clean 4-column configurations alongside curated 3-up product blocks with discovery anchor panels.

### Layout Modules
- **Hero & Campaign Strips:** Full viewport-width bleed with inner safe-zone typography aligned to the grid edge.
- **Top 10 Strip:** Horizontal overflow scroll on mobile with sequential index numerics (`1` through `10`) underlaid beneath cards; fluid grid row on desktop.
- **Trust & Metric Grid:** 3-column data callout strip enclosed with generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` top/bottom).

## Elevation & Depth

The design system is predominantly **flat and architectural**, relying on tonal surface changes and structural grid lines rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - Base canvas: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Secondary editorial containers: `#F7F6F2` and `#EDEBE7`.
  - Floating UI & Modals: Crisp `#FFFFFF` with ultra-diffuse ambient elevation (`0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Overlays & Badges:** Product badges and image hover buttons utilize semi-translucent backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with subdued opacity (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), anchoring elements directly inside photography without visual heaviness.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Extremely fine, low-contrast separator lines (`#E5E5E5`, 1px solid) to structure sub-navigation bars, drawers, and metric dividers without breaking visual continuity.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric shape language. Sharp right angles and soft micro-radii blend modern editorial discipline with functional touch affordances:

- **Cards & Media:** Strict rectangular forms (`0px` border-radius) for product images, campaign banners, and full-width containers to maintain high-fashion editorial purity.
- **Interactive Badges & CTAs:** Micro-rounded corners (`2px` to `4px`) for primary buttons and text badges, ensuring they feel crisp and architectural rather than toy-like.
- **Category Filter Tabs & Pills:** Subtle rounded-sm (`4px`) or pill contours (`999px`) reserved strictly for sub-navigation chips (`READY TO WEAR`, `UNSTITCHED`, `WEST`) and round numeric indicator badges.

## Components

### 1. Product Cards
- **Aspect Ratio:** Portrait `3:4` or `2:3` high vertical image aspect ratios. Borderless perimeter.
- **Image Treatment:** Clean photography with full coverage. Subtle smooth zoom on desktop cursor hover (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Merchandising Badges:** Fixed at top-left/top-right of image. Compact pill or rectangle format, uppercase, `badge-label` token. Dark translucent fill (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`) with white text for scarcity states (`LIMITED`, `BEST SELLER`, `SELLING FAST`). Light neutral (`#F3F3F3`) for fit states (`MODERN FIT`).
- **Product Details:** Placed directly below the image with zero enclosing borders.
  - Line 1: Collection / Silhouette Name (`subheading`, `#222222`, single-line clamp).
  - Line 2: Pricing in PKR (`price-primary`, `Rs. 2,490`).
  - Line 3 (Optional): Small rating stars string (`★★★★★ 4.8`) and discrete color swatch circles (`12px` diameter with 1px border).

### 2. Category Sub-Navigation Tabs
- Centered inline links or subtle pills beneath collection titles (e.g. `READY TO WEAR | UNSTITCHED | WEST | DRESSES`).
- Active state indicated by solid black underline (`2px`) or solid `#000000` pill with white text; inactive state in muted charcoal (`#767676`).

### 3. Campaign & Feature Banners
- Full-width or asymmetric 2-column editorial layouts pairing campaign portraiture with an off-center headline stack.
- Includes category eyebrows in all-caps, an editorial serif statement line, supporting body copy, and a rectangular stark black button (`Shop Now →`).

### 4. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#000000` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `2px` micro-radius, uppercase tracked typography (`eyebrow-caps`), height `44px` or `48px`. Hover shifts to deep charcoal `#222222`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pure white background, `1px solid #000000`, black text.
- **Deep-Link Discovery Tile:** The final slot in 4-column product rows (e.g. `Discover 1140 More ↗ Explore`) featuring blurred contextual photography beneath an ecru frosted center pill button.

### 5. Social Proof & Metric Strip
- Warm ecru background (`#F7F6F2`).
- 3-column metric layout showcasing bold numbers (`3.2M+`, `94%`, `200+`) paired with light secondary text.
- Horizontal sub-strip with inline trust markers: `Easy 30-day exchange`, `Secure checkout`, `Ships across Pakistan`, `Cash on delivery available`.

### 6. Dense Enterprise Footer
- Full-width stark black (`#000000`) background with pure white typography.
- Structured hierarchy: Purpose manifesto block, dynamic multi-column link lists (`HELP`, `ABOUT`, `PAYMENT METHOD`), localized customer service operational hours, accepted payment icons (Visa, Mastercard, PayFast), and copyright notice.
